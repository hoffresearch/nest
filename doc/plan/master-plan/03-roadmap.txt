phase: FLAGSHIP-PROVEN GATE
hard gate between the flagship and pillars 5-8
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
goal:         NOTHING past this point gets code until BOTH conditions hold: (1) the headline number is
              proven and published - the micro preset (dict + fsst + block/rotational int8, landed
              pre-gate) measured well below the current tiny 0.283 on ONE real,
              named, license-clean corpus with recall@10 held by the exact rerank; (2) the
              agent-native flagship has external users. this gate is structural, not
              aspirational: it converts the discipline from 'build everything additively' into
              'build the wedge, prove it, then earn the rest.'

  * [cross] gate criteria (release-check enforced)
    owns:   doc/plan/master-plan/gate.txt

    from:   embeddings-benchmark/mteb, jvm-repo-rebuild/reproducible-central
    risk:   if the gate is skipped, the solo author rebuilds the ten-mediocre-products trap the discipline
            section exists to prevent. the gate is the single most important missing artifact
            from the draft.
