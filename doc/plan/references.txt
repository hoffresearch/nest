----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. the gate IS the discipline        (informs: tasks/gate/10 and the whole roadmap order)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
the owner's cognitive profile names the failure mode directly: "cataloging sometimes substitutes
for creating", and "builds fundamental layers, never the final application". the precedents it
lists (otlet's mundaneum, nelson's xanadu, kircher) are full of visionaries who mapped
everything and shipped little; the one who shipped (devine lu linvega) is flagged as the
outlier. the adversarial critic reached the same verdict independently, with no sight of the
profile.

takeaway: the flagship-proven gate is not bureaucracy. it is the countermeasure to the single
pattern most likely to kill nest. honor the cataloging (the map precedes the territory, it is
real cognition), but keep the gate sacred: prove the wedge on one real corpus, ship it, then
earn the rest. when in doubt, ship the smaller thing.


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. the lens viewer may already exist in lua   (informs: tasks/gate/16, the cosmos.gl viewer)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
the owner has a pure-lua forceatlas2 + barnes-hut graph engine: i/o-agnostic, runs identically
in luajit (cli/bench) and wasmoon (browser), struct-of-arrays zero-alloc hot loop, with
pagerank / ego / filter. it composes cleanly with nest's planned graph-export json (the engine
consumes nodes + an edges_of function): nest exports the graph, the lua engine lays it out and
renders. this is MORE sovereign than cosmos.gl (owner's code, no heavy dep) and fits "lens
lives outside the sovereign core".

scale caveat, taken from the owner's own code comments: the wasmoon buffer bridge does
per-element setValue (2N calls/frame), good to ~1e3-1e4 nodes; for millions of nodes the bridge
is the ceiling, and the fix is named there too (a C trampoline via addFunction = one memcpy per
frame, or a gpu transform-feedback path). so: strong candidate for the lens core, and the
viewer work-order should budget the bridge trampoline / gpu path before claiming scale.


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. two ways to address: content-hash vs human-mnemonic   (informs: tasks/p1/08, the graph pillar)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
nest addresses by content-hash (nest://hash/chunk: machine truth, dedup, integrity). the owner's
3ch registry addresses by human mnemonic (3-char codes decoded in the head: iata, cdn, asm: low
cognitive load). the 3ch 'gap' note frames the same split (wikidata opaque/numeric q42 vs 3ch
mnemonic). nest today sits firmly in the opaque/cryptographic camp.

opportunity (future, NOT now): nest could carry BOTH: the content-hash layer (integrity, dedup)
plus an OPTIONAL human-mnemonic alias layer over the hash. that marries the two owner obsessions
in one place: compression/integrity AND low-cognitive-load addressing. also worth borrowing from
3ch when the graph/agent-native node schema firms up: nodes are flat (no folders),
self-documenting (a 'nod' node describes the schema of the others), and every node carries real
source urls (rep/doc/dat/hug/src) - which is exactly the agent-native + dataset-catalog material
nest wants to serve. do not act on this pre-gate; it is a north-star note.


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. who this is for                    (informs: the verticals and the whole product feel)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
the three artifacts converge on one audience: the compulsive cataloger, the convergence-thinker,
the visual-spatial mind, the fundamental-layer builder, often neurodivergent (the
devine / matuschak / brander / borges lineage). that is the same person as the pattern-lover
vertical, and it is the owner. nest is not a generic database; it is the instrument for this
specific, real, underserved cognitive type. keep the ux low-cognitive-load, graph-first, and
small-file by default. that is the soul, and it is coherent end to end.
