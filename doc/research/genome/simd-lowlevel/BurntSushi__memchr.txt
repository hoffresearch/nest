what:        A Rust crate providing CPU-feature-dispatched SIMD byte-search (memchr/2/3) and substring search
             (memmem) built on a write-once `Vector` trait abstraction, a frequency-ranked
             "packed pair" rare-byte prefilter, the Two-Way string-matching algorithm as a
             linear-time fallback, and a SWAR (SIMD-within-a-register) scalar path.
innovation:  The "packed pair" rare-byte prefilter combined with a write-once generic Vector trait: choose
             the two statistically-rarest needle bytes from a 256-entry background frequency
             rank table, broadcast and SIMD-scan both positions, AND the movemasks to get
             near-zero-false-positive candidates, verify with Two-Way, and dynamically
             self-disable the prefilter when it stops being selective -- all written once over a
             tiny Vector trait with thin AVX2/NEON/SSE2/wasm/SWAR shims.
techniques:
  - Packed-pair prefilter with two rarest needle bytes + AND of movemasks
  - 256-entry HeuristicFrequencyRank background byte-frequency table
  - PrefilterState dynamic false-positive-driven disabling
  - Two-Way O(m+n)/O(1) verifier backbone
  - Rabin-Karp small-haystack fast path
  - Write-once generic Vector trait with per-arch shims
  - SWAR has_zero_byte bit-twiddle + splat broadcast
  - Runtime AVX2/SSE2 feature dispatch with reusable amortized Finder
nest steals: Two concrete wins, both in nest-runtime. (1) bm25_index (0x08) and search-text: the bm25
             tokenizer/term scan and any substring/term lookup over chunks_canonical can use a
             memchr-style packed-pair prefilter to scan mmapped text without allocation; this
             directly attacks the known CJK/Thai degradation by letting a future codepoint-aware
             scanner stay vectorized. Practically, depend on the `memchr` crate (zero-dep,
             no_std-capable) in nest-runtime rather than hand-rolling -- it respects the
             never-open-a-socket / offline constraint and is deterministic. (2) The bigger steal
             is the architectural pattern for nest's simd dispatcher in nest-runtime: memchr's
             write-once `Vector` trait + per-target shim (AVX2/NEON/scalar) is exactly the shape
             nest's dot-product dispatcher already wants; adopt it so the f32/f16/int8 cosine
             kernels are one generic body with trivial AVX2/NEON/SWAR shims instead of three
             parallel copies, keeping files under the 300-line cap and removing duplication. The
             SWAR has_zero_byte/splat trick is a ready-made deterministic scalar fallback
             pattern for any byte-level scan added to nest-format (e.g.
             validating/section-scanning during reader.rs parsing) that must never panic and
             must run identically on every arch.
compression: Indirect but real. memchr is not a compressor, so it does not shrink the .nest file. Its
             relevance to the compress-more-than-everyone goal is on the SERVE side: faster,
             allocation-free scanning over compressed/mmapped text sections means nest can
             afford to keep more sections zstd-compressed and still hit latency targets, because
             term/substring lookup over decoded text stays in vectorized loops. The packed-pair
             + frequency-rank idea is also directly transferable to building selective
             prefilters over int8-quantized embedding scans (pick the most discriminative
             dimensions first), which helps keep the tiny preset (int8/hnsw) fast at high
             compression ratio.
perf:        Throughput is dominated by staying in the vector loop: AVX2 processes 32 bytes/iter,
             SSE2/NEON/wasm 16 bytes/iter, scalar SWAR 8 bytes (usize) per iter. The rare-byte
             pair keeps false positives near zero so verification is rarely triggered; the
             PrefilterState self-disable prevents pathological O(n*m)-ish blowups on adversarial
             inputs. Zero heap allocation in the hot path, no_std-capable (SSE2/NEON without
             std, AVX2 gated behind std feature detection). Reusable Finder amortizes searcher
             construction across many calls -- a measurable win over libc memmem and std which
             reconstruct per call.
